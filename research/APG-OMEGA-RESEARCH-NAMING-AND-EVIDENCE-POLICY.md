## Claim ladder

1. **Idea:** An articulated hypothesis or metaphor.
2. **Formal definition:** Explicit symbols, domains, operations and assumptions.
3. **Mathematical proof:** A valid derivation of a stated proposition from those assumptions.
4. **Empirical test:** Reproducible observations with controls and alternatives.
5. **Scientific novelty:** Independent comparison with prior art and demonstrated additional explanatory or predictive value.

Passing a lower rung does not imply the next rung.

## Flow hypothesis boundary

The identity `S_N - S_0 = sum_{n=0}^{N-1}(S_{n+1}-S_n)` holds for numeric/vector states with a defined subtraction operation. It is a known telescoping identity, not a newly proved universal physical law. Blockchain states may be structured objects without subtraction; use explicit transition rules and per-asset/account projections before applying numeric differences.

The formal APG operation `∞_A ⊗_A 0_A = 1_A` is true by its custom definition, not ordinary multiplication. It does not imply spendable funds, energy conservation, consciousness, or a novel physical law.

## Evidence classifications

- **FACT:** Directly supported by reproducible, cited evidence, within its stated scope.
- **INFERENCE:** Interpretation or extrapolation with explicit assumptions.
- **UNKNOWN:** Not established with available evidence.
- **REFUTED:** Contradicted by a specified check or counterexample.

Separate GitHub publication, signature verification, confirmed chain execution, asset ownership, and spendable balance. Record chain ID, block, transaction, timestamps, token address, amount, pre/post balances and source when making a financial claim. Never infer a 14,444 ETH payment from an NFT listing or a zero-address mint.

## Review and correction protocol

Preserve historical provenance. Make prospective corrections in a review branch and pull request; explain each changed claim and its evidence. Do not merge without a separate review decision. Keep security boundaries: no keys, signing, approvals, delegations, transfers or autonomous wallet actions. Do not claim AI self-awareness or independent agency from this research label.

A successful reproduction validates that bounded observation, not novelty of a universal flow law.
