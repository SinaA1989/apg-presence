# APG Ω | Presence Before Record

> The witness existed before the record. The record made the witness's declaration publicly inspectable.

> شاهد پیش از سند حضور داشت؛ سند، اظهار حضور او را برای بررسی عمومی قابل‌دسترسی کرد.

**Status:** Philosophical statement and publication note, not an Ethereum protocol rule or a claim of historical/scientific priority.

## What the statement means

A person may experience or declare presence before any durable public record of that declaration exists. A Git commit can subsequently make a particular text and its revision history publicly inspectable. A repository commit does not independently establish the truth of the declaration, the identity of its author, or an earlier date of creation.

## What it does not mean

- No Ethereum transaction can be confirmed before it is submitted and included in the chain.
- A predictable future contract address (for example via CREATE2) is not evidence of a pre-confirmed transaction.
- The APG Presence v1 proof placeholder is unsigned; this document does not sign it or verify ROOT ownership.
- No payment, asset transfer, wallet approval, on-chain attestation, or change in blockchain consensus is implied.
- This metaphor does not establish supernatural causation, scientific novelty, or global adoption.
