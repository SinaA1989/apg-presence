# APG | Curated History of Documented Ideas (v1)

**Scope:** an evidence-conscious index of ideas previously expressed in APG discussions. This is **not** a claim that the ideas were invented first, scientifically validated, implemented, or recorded on-chain. The dates below identify *known discussion references*, not priority or invention dates. Exact original transcripts are not published here; independent readers cannot authenticate conversation provenance from this file alone. Dates and quotations should be cross-checked against original records before use in a formal priority claim.

**Publication standard:** A documented *proposal* may be accurately reported as a proposal. Only externally reproducible facts should be labeled *verified implementation*. Do not convert hypotheses, symbolic expressions, or financial hopes into factual achievements.

## Exclusions from verified achievements

No cancer cure, universal medical treatment, definitive religious interpretation, guaranteed revenue, unprecedented global mathematical discovery, Ethereum consensus change, or supernatural event is asserted here. These have no adequate supporting evidence for inclusion as verified achievements.

## Maintenance and provenance

1. Preserve the difference between **idea discussed**, **document published**, **code tested**, **wallet signature verified**, **on-chain execution**, and **real-world outcome**.
2. Add exact source URLs, commit hashes, block/transaction identifiers and timestamps only after checking them.
3. Avoid personal identifying details, private messages, seed phrases and unverifiable personal attribution.
4. Correct or withdraw an entry when original records contradict it.
5. The archive is a **selective first edition**, not an exhaustive reconstruction of years of conversations.

**Truth before claims. Responsibility before power. Verification before trust.**
