Independently inspect the displayed message, recovered signer address, and verification status on Etherscan before attributing a particular text to ROOT. A signed message can evidence control of a signing key for the message; it is not a blockchain transaction, financial transfer, on-chain timestamp, proof of human identity, or proof of current key access.

The historical Etherscan messages have different formats and must **not** be copied into it as if they were fresh, unexpired APG Presence v1 signatures. This publication does not change that status.
